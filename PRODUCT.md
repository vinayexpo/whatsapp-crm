# Creative Connects

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Creative Connects connects customer messaging, AI assistance, automation, CRM, campaigns, voice, and commerce in one business workspace.

## Users

Business teams managing customer conversations and operations. The public landing page prioritizes AI and automation, as requested by the user.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing routes cover WhatsApp and Instagram inbox, contacts, phonebooks, pipeline, campaigns, analytics, automations, AI assistant, chatbots, chat menus, templates, voice agents, WhatsApp calling, branches, catalog, orders, reports, and settings. Availability depends on account role and configured integrations. Sign-in exists; public self-service registration and published pricing are not established.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the existing Creative Connects name, teal theme, Manrope body text, Space Grotesk headings, and Material UI icon language.

## Evidence on Hand

Feature evidence comes from app/routes.ts and the corresponding screens. Public previews use labelled sample data. No verified testimonials, customer logos, adoption numbers, or performance guarantees have been supplied.
