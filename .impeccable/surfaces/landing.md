## Current finite-motion revision

The landing hero uses the same illustrative Enquiry → AI assistant → Your team graphic as the industry previews, keyed by selected mode and sample. It automatically runs once on entering view and settles after 4.2 seconds: three 1.4-second dash cycles, one 4.2-second pass for each packet, and stage changes every 1.4 seconds. A different mode or sample starts a fresh run; selecting the current choice does not restart it. Offscreen and hidden-document checks suspend the graphic, and returning to view does not restart a completed run. Reduced motion displays the static final stage.

The voice panel automatically animates its waveform for four 0.8-second iterations, with staggered delays up to 1.26 seconds; the last bar settles within 4.46 seconds. Reduced motion leaves the waveform static. The visible transcript and explicit no-audio/no-calls statement remain available. Voice motion is illustrative and has no playback controls. Existing public colors, typography, shell, and components remain the visual authority; this is an ordinary incumbent-theme extension.
