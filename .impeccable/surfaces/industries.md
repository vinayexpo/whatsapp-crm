## Interaction and routing

- Each sector has three labelled sample tabs. Click, ArrowLeft, ArrowRight, Home, and End select a scenario and move focus; only the selected tab enters the tab order. The selected tab identifies its panel, and the changing conversation is announced politely. Each scenario changes the customer question, illustrative AI answer, and three workflow steps. Navigating to another sector resets the sample through a sector-keyed component.
- The directory's labelled native select changes the sector used by its workflow preview. Both previews show an inline SVG path from Enquiry to AI assistant to Your team, with a readable current-step caption. Connection dashes flow on a 1.8-second cycle; packets loop over 7.2 seconds while a timer advances three stages every 2.4 seconds. Sector answers enter over 280ms when their sample changes. Motion styles run only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.
- Labelled keyboard-operable controls have 44px targets for pause/play and replay. A single running state gates both CSS playback and the stage timer; intersection and document-visibility checks stop them offscreen or when the document is hidden. Replay resets the stage and SVG animation. Reduced-motion mode remains still and offers a labelled next-step control; replay resets it without enabling animation. These are illustrative vector graphics, with no actual messages or external operations.
- FAQs use native `details` / `summary` disclosures. Shared mobile navigation exposes expanded state, closes after selecting a link, and supports Escape with focus restored to its toggle. Existing public focus and reduced-motion behavior remains in force.
- Directory cards, breadcrumbs, related sectors, shared navigation/footer, and the landing page's eleven sector links connect the public routes. Product and automation links point back to the matching landing anchors. Sign-in actions lead to `/login`; no public registration or trial flow is invented.
- Legacy slugs `schools`, `clinics`, and `salons` resolve to the renamed sectors and use a React Router `Navigate` replacement to `education-edtech`, `healthcare`, and `spas-salons`, preserving the URL hash. Their metadata uses the canonical sector URL. This is a client route redirect; no HTTP redirect status is claimed.
- Unknown sector slugs show a helpful industry-browser fallback within the shared public shell, with `robots: noindex` and an industry-not-found title. This is a rendered fallback, not a documented HTTP 404 response.
- The directory provides title, description, and canonical metadata. Valid sector pages add sector-specific title, description, Open Graph title/description, and canonical URL under `https://creativeconnects.web.id/industries/`. `app/routes.ts` registers the directory and parameterized route; `public/sitemap.xml` includes the directory and eleven canonical sector URLs.

## Truth and capability boundaries

The demonstration is labelled sample and illustrative, and explicitly states that it sends no messages and books no appointments. Tabs only switch local example content. Examples explain the product's configurable AI knowledge, shared inbox, chat menus, assignments, contacts, campaigns, CRM, and commerce capabilities; they do not imply that a sector-specific operational system already exists. Actual WhatsApp use requires the relevant business account, permissions, and channel setup. Campaigns depend on eligible audiences, consent, and template requirements.

- Education & Edtech: staff confirm admissions and course information; learning-platform access, student records, attendance, and fee collection remain in existing education systems.
- Healthcare: examples cover administrative enquiries; staff confirm appointments. No diagnosis, medical advice, clinical-record integration, or compliance certification is implied.
- Restaurants: ordering, delivery, and payment behavior depends on configured services; staff confirm dietary questions and changes.
- Real estate: agents confirm property availability, prices, and visits; no built-in listing feed or visit calendar is promised.
- Spas & Salons: staff confirm appointment requests; no stylist calendar or automatic booking is implied.
- Gyms: staff confirm tours, terms, and prices; no class booking, access-control integration, or personalized fitness advice is implied.
- Ecommerce: catalog, inventory, delivery, and payments depend on configuration; no automatic marketplace synchronization or abandoned-cart recovery is implied.
- Events & Webinar: staff supply event details and confirm registration; ticketing, hosting, and attendance remain in existing services unless separately integrated.
- IT Services & Internet: staff confirm coverage and availability; diagnostics, provisioning, billing, and ticket systems require existing tools or separate integrations.
- HR & Recruitment: staff control hiring decisions and communication; applicant tracking, background checks, payroll, and interview calendars remain in existing HR systems.
- Banking & Fintech: examples explain general services, without financial advice or account transactions. Banking operations, identity checks, and payments require approved systems; no compliance certification is implied.
