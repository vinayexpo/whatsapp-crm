# Creative Connects

Creative Connects is an AI-powered customer communication and business management application. It brings WhatsApp and Instagram conversations, automation, CRM, campaigns, voice and commerce into one workspace.

## Key Features

- **AI assistance and automation:** Answer common questions using business knowledge, build chatbot conversations, create guided menus and hand conversations over to your team.
- **Shared inbox:** Manage WhatsApp and Instagram messages with conversation history, attachments, team assignments and delivery status.
- **Contacts and CRM:** Organize contacts with tags and phonebooks, import CSV or Excel files, and track leads through sales stages.
- **Campaigns and templates:** Select audiences using phonebooks or tags, draft utility and marketing templates with your company's AI provider, edit copy and sample values, submit templates for Meta review, schedule campaigns and review delivery results.
- **Voice and calling:** Use configured AI voice agents, call workflows and WhatsApp calling to support customer enquiries and follow-up.
- **Commerce:** Set up fulfillment locations, products, inventory and delivery. Sync product details, prices and availability with Meta catalogs in both directions, review conflicts, and manage WhatsApp orders.
- **WhatsApp payments:** Configure company-specific India/Razorpay checkout, send payment requests, verify provider receipts and track payment status.
- **Meta Ads:** Connect an ad account, generate editable campaign drafts with your configured AI assistant, and build single-image Facebook and Instagram ads for website visits or WhatsApp conversations. Pull Meta changes automatically, review conflicting edits, approve supported changes back to Meta, publish paused and review performance reports. Requires approved Meta permissions and connected business assets.
- **Analytics:** Review conversation activity, campaign performance, sales pipeline and commerce reports.
- **Team management:** Manage team members, roles, permissions and company settings.
- **Platform management:** Superadmins manage companies, users, account status, usage and activity history.

## Who Can Use It?

- **Business owners and managers** overseeing customer communication, sales and operations.
- **Sales and support teams** handling enquiries, leads and customer follow-up.
- **Marketing teams** organizing audiences and running messaging campaigns.
- **Platform administrators** managing companies and user access.
